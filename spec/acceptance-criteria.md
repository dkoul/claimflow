# Acceptance criteria

- All three demo users can log in and log out.
- Each role sees the correct dashboard metrics and navigation.
- Employees create, edit, submit, and resubmit claims.
- Managers approve and reject pending claims assigned to them.
- Finance reimburses approved claims.
- Invalid transitions and unauthorized access are rejected by the API.
- Claim history records lifecycle events.
- List search, filter, sort, and pagination work.
- Reset script restores original demo data.
- Specifications in `spec/` match implemented behavior.
